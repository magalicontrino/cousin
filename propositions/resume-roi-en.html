Updated: 24-02-25

GENERAL INFORMATION

  • This document is a summary of the internal rules (ROI).
  • The ROI sets out your rights and your obligations for using our services.
  • The Prince de Liège centre is an emergency shelter providing temporary accommodation and care for homeless people.
  • The services offered here are free of charge.
  • The centre door closes at 9.30 p.m.
  • The number to call if you need to reach the centre (if you are late, if you have questions, …) is 02.329.05.94 (reception) or 0800.99.340 (Samusocial telephone dispatch).
  • Nights spent outside the centre during your stay are only possible with the agreement of the PMS team and under certain conditions, which are set out in the general ROI.
  • If these conditions are not respected, Samusocial may decide to end your accommodation.

YOUR ARRIVAL

  • When you join the project, you will be asked to meet a social worker and to hand your medication in to the infirmary.
  • The centre and its facilities will be shown to you.
  • We ask you to respect the rules of the centre (as set out in the general ROI) in order to use our services.

YOUR DEPARTURE

  • Except in exceptional situations, you will be told one week in advance that your stay here is coming to an end. You will be asked to return the items lent to you by Samusocial to the staff, and to clean your room.
  • The centre may keep some of your belongings for up to 14 days after you leave.

COMMUNAL SERVICES

  • Several services are available to you. (Bed, meals, washrooms, television …).
  • You must respect the opening hours of these services in order to use them.
  • A WIFI connection is available (network: Samusocial Guest). The password is S@mUfree2020.
  • The centre can lend you a telephone and a computer for your administrative procedures.
  • A locker is also available to you, to keep some of your belongings safe.
  • All the services we offer are listed in the general ROI of the centre.

PMS SERVICES

  • A psycho-medico-social team is available to support you throughout your stay.
  • You are expected to work together with this team.
  • Refusing to work with the social team may bring your support to an end.
  • Refusing to be referred to accommodation suited to your situation may lead to a decision to end your stay.
  • If there is a risk of contagion, the medical team may ask you to take certain steps or to accept specific care.
  • Refusing care, where there is a risk of contagion, may bring your stay to an end.

RULES FOR LIVING TOGETHER

  • Violence of any kind is strictly forbidden and will be sanctioned automatically.
  • Using or dealing drugs, theft and vandalism are forbidden.
  • Drinking alcohol on site is forbidden.
  • Smoking is only allowed in the area provided for that purpose.
  • You are asked to keep the whole building and its immediate surroundings clean.
  • Eating is only allowed in the dining room and the lounge, apart from fruit and dry food, provided hygiene is maintained.
  • To avoid any risk of fire, having or using equipment that draws a lot of electricity, or flammable material, is strictly forbidden in the centre.
  • Cooking in your room is strictly forbidden.
  • Sharp or blunt objects (knives, hammers, …) are forbidden in the centre.
  • For safety and hygiene reasons, staff carry out regular rounds of the rooms. If staff find a dangerous object, it will be removed and given back to its owner on the strict condition that it is taken out of the centre.
  • Pets are not allowed in the centre.
  • For hygiene reasons, luggage is limited to 3 boxes per person.
  • Out of respect for everyone's modesty, you are asked to be suitably dressed when moving around the centre and its surroundings.
  • So that everyone can rest, you are asked to avoid noise from 9 p.m., particularly in your room.

SANCTIONS

  • If you break the rules set out in the general internal rules, a sanction may be taken against you.
  • The list of sanctions that apply when the ROI is not respected is given in the general ROI of the project.

CONFIDENTIALITY

  • Staff, as well as students and volunteers, are bound by a duty of confidentiality.
  • This means that information about you will only be passed on to outside partners with your agreement.
  • The PMS team is bound by professional secrecy.
  • There are cameras in the centre, filming the communal areas.
  • Visits from people outside the centre are forbidden, both inside and in the immediate surroundings.
  • Taking a photo or a video of another person in the centre without their prior agreement is strictly forbidden.

COMPLAINTS PROCEDURE

  • If you suffer any abuse from one of our staff, volunteers or students, Samusocial encourages you to make a complaint to the coordination team of your project.
  • You can also send your complaint to Vivalis through their website: https://www.vivalis.brussels/fr/plainte
